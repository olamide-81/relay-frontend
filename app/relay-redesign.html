The problem

Provider research is broken.

A new KYC, payout, or FX provider launches every week. Keeping up is a full-time job nobody hires for.

01

Too many providers

Across KYC, payouts, FX, treasury, compliance, collections, and lending, the infrastructure map doubles every 18 months — and nobody is keeping score.

02

No way to compare

Pricing lives behind NDAs. Uptime lives in status pages. Integration docs live behind sales calls. Every real comparison is reconstructed from scratch.

03

Shipping takes months

Research, compliance review, API sandbox, test transactions, go-live sign-off. By the time you ship, the market has already moved.

The thesis

Bridges, not silos.

Relay sits one layer above the fragmentation — connecting the providers you can already reach with the ones you haven't found yet. Across Africa, LATAM, Europe, and the world.

A note from the team

Why we are building Relay.

We have integrated KYC in five African markets, payout rails in three LATAM ones, and compliance tooling across Europe. Every time, the research phase — pitch decks, sales calls, NDAs, Slack threads — took longer than the actual integration. That felt backwards.

Relay is the directory we wished existed when we were shipping. One layer above the fragmentation, priced to be useful, and never selling your research activity to the providers you are comparing.
