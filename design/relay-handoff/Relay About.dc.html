About

Financial infrastructure is the most important market nobody could see.

Every fintech is built on partners: a bank that holds the money, a Provider that moves it, another that verifies who it belongs to. Choosing them used to mean months of cold emails, outdated spreadsheets and sales calls.

Gratebridge Labs started Relay in 2024 to make that market legible. Today we map 1,600 infrastructure partners across 140 countries, verified every week, and help teams at every stage find the right ones faster.
